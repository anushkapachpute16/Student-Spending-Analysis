## Checks that failed during the build and were corrected

* Excel recalculation: LibreOffice kept XlsxWriter's stored zero results instead of recalculating, so the first comparison reported 303 of 306 mismatches. Fixed by recalculating a copy with no cached results under a forced-recalculation profile; after that all 306 formula cells matched.
* Unit test failure (test_all_queries_parse_and_match_pandas): the pandas reference for SQL query Q10 assumed all three payment methods exist and raised KeyError on a fixture with two. Fixed by reindexing to the three methods with zero fill (and a richer fixture).
* Matplotlib 3.11 deprecation of boxplot(vert=False): replaced by a helper that uses orientation='horizontal' and falls back for older versions.
* Correlation heatmap: the 1.00 diagonal dominated the colour scale. Redrawn as the lower triangle without the diagonal.
