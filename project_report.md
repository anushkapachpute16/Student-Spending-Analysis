## 10. Statistical methods

* **Descriptive:** count, mean, median, mode, min/max, range, sample variance and standard deviation (n-1), quartiles/percentiles (linear interpolation), IQR, frequencies and percentages (denominator = non-missing rows).
* **Outliers:** Tukey fences (Q1 - 1.5 IQR, Q3 + 1.5 IQR).
* **Group comparisons:** permutation one-way ANOVA (F statistic, 10,000 shuffles, effect size eta-squared) for means; permutation chi-square test (effect size Cramer's V) for two categorical variables. Permutation tests need no normality assumption and no SciPy; they assume independent observations and exchangeable labels under the null hypothesis.
* **Correlation:** Pearson r for every pair of the 13 numeric variables, with permutation p-values (5,000 shuffles) and a bootstrap 95% interval for the income-aid correlation.
* **Multiple testing:** Holm step-down correction, applied separately to the 7 planned group tests and to the 78 correlation pairs. Significance level alpha = 0.05. Random seed 42 (results are reproducible).
* **Interpretation rules:** a p-value is the probability of a result at least this extreme *if the null hypothesis were true*, not the probability that the null is true. 'Not significant' means 'no detectable difference', not 'proven equal'. Correlation is not causation. The sampling method is unknown, so nothing here is claimed to generalise to all college students.

## 17. Practical recommendations

1. **Do not use this dataset to judge affordability, savings or financial stress** until the unit and period of each column is documented.
2. **Document provenance** (source, collection method, sampling, licence) before publishing the data or presenting findings as real-world evidence.
3. **Collect expenses on a common period** (for example monthly, with tuition spread over the months it covers) if the analysis is to compare income with spending.
4. **Report the null results as results:** no detectable differences by major, gender or year, and no correlations, is itself a finding worth stating.

## 18. Limitations

* Currency and period of every amount are undocumented; no monthly spending or savings is calculated.
* Source, sampling and licence unknown; results describe this file and may not generalise.
* Near-flat distributions limit what descriptive statistics such as the mode can tell.
* Permutation tests assume independent observations; this cannot be checked from the file.
* Seven group tests and 78 correlations were run; Holm correction was applied, but exploratory choices (which groups to compare) were made after seeing summary tables.
* SQL execution was verified on SQLite, not MySQL. Excel formulas were verified in LibreOffice, not Microsoft Excel.
